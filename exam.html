Mock exam

Sit a full paper, then find out where the marks went

A fresh paper every time, drawn across all ten topics. Answer everything, submit once, and the paper is marked immediately — with a grade, a topic breakdown, and a full worked solution for every question.

Before you start

  • 14 questions spread across all ten topics, mixed Skill, Apply and Challenge.
  • Only the final answer is marked, so write your working on paper as you would in the real exam.
  • The QCAA formula sheet is allowed — open it in a second tab.
  • There is no time limit, but the clock runs so you can see your pace.
  • You can move between questions freely and change answers until you submit.

How it is graded

Your percentage of the total marks available sets the grade.

GradePercentage

These bands are for your own tracking. QCAA reports subject results on its own scale and does not use plus/minus grades, so treat this as a progress check rather than a prediction of an exam result.
